The problem

Social shouldn't feel like a second job.

Switching apps, missing slots, and guessing what worked is how a feed goes quiet. One calendar, one inbox, one report — built for posting, not for queues or reviews.

Fragmented publishing

The same update is rebuilt in Instagram, Facebook, and Google. What went live never matches the plan you thought you had.

Inconsistent cadence

Busy weeks go silent. Followers drift because nobody had time to babysit three queues when the floor was full.

Blind performance

Insights sit in four dashboards. You cannot tell which caption, time, or channel actually drove the visit.

What you get

Everything you need to look professional every week.

Publish once, everywhere

Plan Instagram, Facebook, Google, and more from a single dashboard instead of repeating the same post in every app.

Set the week on autopilot

See what's actually working

Reach, clicks, and growth sit in one report so you can double down on the posts that bring people in.

Write captions in seconds

Reply from one inbox
