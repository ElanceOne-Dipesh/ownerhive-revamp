About OwnerHive

Everything you need to know about the platform, automated operations, and how OwnerHive helps local businesses grow.

OwnerHive is an all-in-one business operations platform built for local business owners. We combine social media management, virtual waitlist queues, web-based customer check-ins, and automated review collection into one connected portal — so you stop bouncing between apps and start running smarter.

Is this helpful?

OwnerHive is built for local business owners — restaurants, salons, spas, fitness studios, retail shops, and service providers — who want to look professional online, reduce customer friction, and grow their reputation without hiring a full marketing team.

Is this helpful?

The Social Manager lets you connect all your social accounts — Instagram, Facebook, Google, and more — and manage everything from one dashboard. You can plan posts in a calendar view, publish with one click, and track engagement without switching apps.

Is this helpful?

No app required — ever. Customers simply text a keyword to join the virtual line, receive live wait-time updates via SMS, and show up when it's their turn. The whole system is browser and SMS-based, so there's zero friction on the customer side.

Is this helpful?

Most businesses are fully live within 30 minutes. Your Web Check-In link is active in under 5 minutes, the Queue Manager is ready to accept customers the same day, and your social accounts connect in seconds. No engineering or technical setup is required.

Is this helpful?

Review Booster automatically sends a review request via SMS or email after each customer visit. Happy customers are guided to Google or other review platforms with one tap. Unhappy customers are routed to a private feedback channel instead, protecting your public reputation before issues go online.
